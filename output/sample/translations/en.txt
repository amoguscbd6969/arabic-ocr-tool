In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful
Sheikh Khamis Ahmad al-Dishi al-Buraiki
Date: 6/5/1425 AH
Corresponding to: 24/6/2004 AD
Attachments
Republic of Yemen - Shabwah Governorate
'Ar'ma' al-Thajjah
The Al Buraik tribe, their stronghold in Shabwah, are:
Muhammad bin Buraik bin Saif bin Hamid bin Sar al-Layl bin Yasn bin Ya Jabr al-Himyari
And his brother Ali bin Buraik, who was transferred to Huraydah and afterwards moved to al-Shihr.
As for Muhammad bin Buraik, he remained in Shabwah, and his descendants are three:
1- Ahmad bin Muhammad bin Buraik (known as Ahmad bin al-Sheikh)
2- Umar bin Muhammad bin Buraik (known as Umar bin al-Sheikh)
3- Ali bin Muhammad bin Buraik (known as Ali bin al-Sheikh)
General division of the sons of Muhammad bin Buraik:
First: Ahmad bin Muhammad bin Buraik and his sons are four:
① Umar bin Ahmad ② Isa bin Ahmad ③ Abu Bakr bin Ahmad ④ Muhammad bin Ahmad
Division of branches as follows, sons of Ahmad bin al-Sheikh:
① Umar bin Ahmad bin al-Sheikh and his sons: a- Ahmad bin Umar bin Muhammad, ancestor of Al Ahmad; b- Muhammad bin Umar bin Muhammad, ancestor of Al Aqil, namely Al Ghawthan and Al Dawud; c- Saif bin Umar bin Muhammad, ancestor of Al Basif.
Second: Isa bin Ahmad bin al-Sheikh, and his sons are: Al Abd al-Rahim and Al Buraid.
Third: Abu Bakr bin Ahmad bin al-Sheikh, and his sons are: Al Najib, Al Abd al-Qawi, al-Bakhnayni, and Al al-Zabar, namely Al Malkum and Al Khudayrul.
Fourth: Muhammad bin Ahmad bin al-Sheikh: died without heirs (kalala). These are the sons of Ahmad bin al-Sheikh.
Second: Umar bin Muhammad bin Buraik and his sons are four: 1- Al Salim bin Umar 2- Al Abdullah bin Umar 3- Al Yusuf bin Umar 4- Al Ahmad bin Umar. These are the sons of Umar bin Muhammad bin al-Sheikh.
Third: Ali bin Muhammad bin Buraik and his sons are two: 1- Ahmad bin Ali, namely Al Ali bin Ahmad; 2- Muhammad bin Ali, namely Al Bam. These are the sons of Ali bin al-Sheikh.
This tree is copied from the list of Sheikh Muhammad bin Abdulrahman bin Muhammad al-Makna al-Dishi.
